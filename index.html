Dashboard Guide

Dashboard Overview

The statistics cards at the top provide an instant snapshot of key healthcare metrics. You can customize the visual accessibility by clicking the Vision Profile button.

Filter & Explore

Use the sidebar to drill down into the data. You can combine multiple filters to isolate specific patient cohorts:

Outcome: Test Results
Diagnosis: Medical Condition
Clinical: Medication Prescribed
Patient: Blood Type, Age, Gender
Admission: Type (Emergency, etc.)
Timeline: Admission Year

Interactive Visualizations

Charts are interactive. Hover over data points for details, or Click elements to focus. The "Advanced Analysis" section contains complex flows like the Parallel Coordinates chart.

Hospital Map

View geographic distribution of hospitals. Markers are colored by the dominant test result. Click a marker to filter the dashboard by that specific hospital.
